How we got here

Three granularity approaches

Same BPE engine; only the unit we pass into training changes. Fresh scores, all 5 languages en/hi/te/sa/mai; vocab 10000, 0 UNK, lossless in every run.

1 · Word-level
Split on spaces → BPE inside each word only.
Feed: India's · population · is · …
Why not best: Indic X > 1.2 → gate fail · score 1374
2 · Sentence-level
Split on sentence ends → whole sentence is one training string (merges can cross spaces).
Feed: India's population is 1,428,627,663.
Why not best: Gates pass, but spread 0.3777 keeps score at 2648
3 · Paragraph-level
Split on newlines → multi-sentence paragraphs as one unit.
Feed: …663. It is the most populous country.
Why not best: Nearly same as sentence (2605) — data, not unit size, was the ceiling
Takeaway
Word → sentence → paragraph: 1374 → 2648 → 2605. Bigger units helped past the gate, then stopped. Cleaning the corpus (instructor dataset) + weight sweep is what unlocked 9953.
