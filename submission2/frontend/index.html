What we were still leaving on the table

Two fair, principled knobs

01

Fractional language upsampling

Deterministic Bresenham line replication. The integer weight grid was too coarse — MAI (the spread ceiling) could never be tuned past 2× even though its fertility was the bottleneck. Fractional weights let each language's fertility be dialed precisely: line i gets ⌊w⌋ copies, plus one whenever the running remainder crosses 1.

Champion weights: EN:2 · HI:2.2 · TE:3.7 · MAI:4.05

02

Newline attachment

Every newline used to be a permanently isolated, unmergeable token — a fertility tax that hit MAI hardest (smallest page, highest relative newline density). Train on line + "\n" and encode in newline-carrying chunks so …\n merges become learnable.

Chunk regex: [^\n]*\n|[^\n]+\Z — each chunk keeps its trailing newline.
